# Prototype Instructions

Run the local server and open the preview in the browser available to this environment. Do not give the user server-start instructions when the environment can run it directly.

Before substantial visual changes, use Product Design context and the latest user-selected screenshots. Record durable prototype-specific decisions here.

## Durable project decisions

- The first viewport is anonymous and work-first. It must not reveal the name, portrait, school, contact details, roles, or biography.
- The intro uses an interactive circular moving-image lens and six project previews. Scrolling reveals the layered monitor work index and personal information.
- The portfolio scroll story uses three intentional phases: a pinned bilingual statement, a real-video frame that rotates and expands toward full viewport, and kinetic typography that separates before the personal-information panel arrives.
- Desktop statement typography must resolve as two open editorial lines with generous surrounding whitespace. The three-line kinetic title uses visible vertical gaps and asymmetric horizontal offsets; mobile removes those offsets and must never split a Chinese phrase mid-word.
- Selected-work media frames enter with alternating scroll-linked offsets and rotation. Mobile keeps the same narrative with lower motion intensity, and reduced-motion mode presents every phase as a readable static sequence.
- The personal portrait is a small framed image in the upper third, never a full-height hero cutout.
- The plan section is `个人计划与学习目的 / PLAN & LEARNING GOALS` and includes: 2028 master's application and portfolio preparation, live-action production combined with AI, and AI-powered personal-IP/social-media creation.
- The selected visual system is a white/light-gray editorial canvas with near-black media frames and `#6643FF` violet interaction accents.
- A sticky segmented section navigator stays at the top and previews Portfolio, Selected Work, About, Capabilities, Social, and Contact.
- Self Evaluation and Professional Skills come from the supplied internship resume, but must be edited into concise bilingual web modules rather than copied as dense resume bullets.
- Professional Skills includes post-production/DIT, generative-AI workflows, video/animation generation, visual/audio assets, LLM/research practice, and English/cross-cultural communication.
- The Professional Skills section is a dark visual stage with oversized, thumbnail-readable hierarchy, staggered capability cards, and a real icon-library toolkit wall for Claude, DaVinci Resolve, Codex, ChatGPT, Gemini, Kling AI, Jimeng AI, Lovart, and Suno.
- Social screenshots must never be embedded. Extracted Xiaohongshu, Douyin, and X information belongs in the integrated Social Desk section on the main scrolling page.
- Each social card keeps its account ID visible and stacks two actions in order: copy the account ID, then open the creator's profile in a new tab.
- The Social Desk uses dedicated still images that do not repeat homepage posters. Its three creative-scene images are Jinan TV studio work, Xiu Mian on-set camera work, and visual motion capture.
- `静默防线 / THE INVISIBLE SHIELD` remains in the portfolio and identifies the role as Producer.
- Chinese copy is visually larger; the English translation sits directly beneath it.
- The About introduction uses punctuation-free phrases in two left-aligned lines, short then long. Narrow columns use three progressively longer lines.
